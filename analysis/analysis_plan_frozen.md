# Analysis plan: multiverse audit of strategy-set fragility (frozen before the audit was run)

Status. This plan was written and saved as a time-stamped project artifact before any multiverse (superset or replacement) computation was executed. It is an internal pre-specification, not an OSF registration. Before freezing, development runs computed (i) the authors'-set and complete-set long-run cooperation for the three anchor configurations of García and Traulsen (2025, PNAS 122, e2319925121) (AllC-TFT-AllD versus reactive versus memory-one; the 4, 9, 12 and 24 strategy punishment sets; stern judging with 3 strategies) in order to verify the implementation; and (ii) an indirect-reciprocity screen that printed, for the norms stern judging (SJ), simple standing (SS), image scoring (IS) and shunning (SH) at b/c = 2, 3 and 5, the long-run cooperation of the authors' set {AllC, AllD, DISC} and of each complete universe (4, 16 and 81 rules). Shunning was screened only at b/c = 2. This screen is therefore multiverse-relevant information that was seen before freezing. Examples of what it showed: SJ at b/c = 2 gave 0.233 with the authors' set and 0.122 with the 81-rule universe; SJ at b/c = 3 gave 0.912 and 0.845; IS at b/c = 5 gave 0.049 and 0.319; SH at b/c = 2 gave 0.227 and 0.107. Two decisions were taken after seeing it. First, the claim that simple standing sustains cooperation was excluded, because it was not reproduced with the authors' set (0.004 to 0.324 for b/c = 2 to 5). Second, the stern-judging regimes were restricted to b/c >= 3, because b/c = 2 did not reproduce the claim with the authors' set. Complete-set values for the retained IR claims (IR1 to IR4 at b/c = 3 and 5) were therefore partly known when the plan was frozen, and the corresponding complete-set verdicts should not be read as blind tests. Superset and replacement SSFI, Shapley and Banzhaf quantities, and all DR and PU claim-level multiverse results were not computed before freezing.

Corpus. Fourteen canonical claims in three model families (direct reciprocity DR, optional public goods with peer punishment PU, indirect reciprocity with public reputations IR), selected because each is a textbook-level conclusion obtained with a hand-picked strategy set whose own state and action description implies a larger complete set, and because each admits exact small-mutation-limit computation. Claims DR1-DR6, PU1-PU4 and IR1-IR4 and their regimes are as coded in `ssfi_code/claims.py` (frozen with this plan). Claim types: level (cooperation above 0.5), negative control (cooperation below 0.5), winner (named strategy more abundant than named rivals), comparative (mechanism arm minus control arm above zero), stability (no strategy in the set fixes with probability above 1/N against the focal resident).

Universes. For every claim three nested admissible universes are pre-defined: U_enc (complete set of the authors' own encoding or monitoring assumptions), U_eq (next computational-equivalence class) and U_max (maximal class). DR: reactive deterministic (8), memory-one deterministic (32), memory-one with probabilities in {0, 1/2, 1} (243); for claims whose authors' set already contains memory-one strategies U_enc = U_eq = memory-one deterministic. PU: 9 (participants may punish participants), 12 (loners may punish, cannot be punished), 24 (everyone may punish everyone). IR: recipient-reputation rules (4), own-and-recipient deterministic rules (16), own-and-recipient rules with probabilities in {0, 1/2, 1} (81).

Primary estimand. SSFI = probability that the claim's verdict reverses when the strategy set is S_A union R, with R uniform over subsets of U \ S_A (each omitted strategy included independently with probability 1/2), conditional on the claim being reproduced with S_A. Exact enumeration when |U \ S_A| <= 16, otherwise 20,000 Monte Carlo draws (seeded). Secondary estimands: replacement SSFI (random sets of size |S_A| containing the claim's core strategies), complete-set verdict (S = U), Shapley attribution of the complete-set margin change to omitted strategies (300 random permutations, primary dynamics), Banzhaf inclusion effects (OLS on inclusion indicators over the superset draws).

Dynamics. Primary D0: pairwise-comparison (Fermi) imitation with the family's population size and beta = 1. Robustness: D1 beta/10, D2 beta*10, D3 birth-death Moran with linear fitness 1 + w (pi - pi_min)/(pi_max - pi_min), w = 1, D4 N/2, D5 2N.

Hypotheses. H1: SSFI increases with the number of neutral-entry strategies in U \ S_A (strategies payoff-neutral with the claim's focal cooperative strategy that are invaded by some strategy of U against which the focal strategy is not invaded). Test: Spearman correlation across reproduced claim-regime-universe cells under D0, with a claim-clustered bootstrap (2,000 resamples) for the confidence interval. H2: stability claims have lower SSFI than long-run-abundance claims (level, winner, comparative). Test: difference in mean SSFI with claim-clustered bootstrap. H3 (secondary): verdict changes across strategy sets are more frequent than verdict changes across the six dynamics with S_A fixed. Field-level summaries: share of reproduced claims whose complete-set verdict reverses under each universe; median SSFI by family and claim type. Negative controls (DR2, IR2) are expected to have low SSFI; a high SSFI there would indicate that the index manufactures fragility.

Validation. Payoff engine against Monte Carlo repeated games; fixation formula against individual-based simulation; small-mutation-limit abundances against finite-mutation individual-based simulation (mutation 1e-3 or smaller) for the three anchors.
